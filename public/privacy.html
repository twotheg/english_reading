Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the ReadFlow: English Reading application (herein referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data we process. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

ReadFlow: English Reading does not require user registration or personal account creation. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

  • Local App Data: Reading preferences, completed reading progress, and local settings are stored directly on your device via browser local storage or indexed databases.
  • Automated & Technical Data: When accessing the application, standard technical logs (e.g., IP address, device model, operating system version, and general network information) may be automatically processed by hosting infrastructure providers (such as Vercel) for server security and operational stability.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

To support the ongoing operation and free availability of the App, we display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob / Google AdSense.

These third-party vendors may use cookies, advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), and other tracking technologies to serve relevant ads based on your prior visits to this App or other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our App and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by adjusting your device's advertising preferences.
  • For more details on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. External Content & News Feeds

The App aggregates and displays content from publicly accessible RSS feeds (e.g., The Conversation, public domain stories). We do not transmit your personal reading behavior or personal data to these external content publishers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences and language learners. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Security

We value your trust. The App communicates strictly over secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols to safeguard data in transit. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact the developer team through the support channel provided on the application's official store listing. twotheg@gmail.com
